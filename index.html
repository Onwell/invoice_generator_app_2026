Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before you start.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Starter plan is free forever and doesn't ask for a card. Pro and Business come with a 14-day trial, and you can cancel before it ends without being charged.

Can I send invoices in multiple currencies?

Yes — multi-currency invoicing is included on the Business plan. Each invoice can use its own currency, and your reports show totals converted to your base currency.

What happens if a client doesn't pay?

Automatic reminders are sent before and after the due date based on the schedule you choose. Invoices that pass the due date are flagged as overdue on your dashboard so nothing slips through.

Can my accountant or teammate log in?

Yes. Invite teammates with roles — admin, accountant, billing clerk or viewer — and control exactly what each person can see and do. Every sign-in is recorded in the audit log.

Is my data exportable?

Always. Export invoices, clients and payments to CSV at any time, and download any invoice as a PDF. Your data belongs to you.

How long does setup take?

About five minutes. Sign up, add your business name and logo, then create your first invoice. There are no onboarding calls or consultants required.
